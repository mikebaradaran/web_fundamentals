Discovery Centre

Mountain Valley

If you really want a good workout, take off on some of our mountain trails which head up onto the top of the range. A hard slog going up, but there is a superb downhill run coming back!

Of course you are welcome to bring your own bike to Mountain Valley, where you can access the trails.

Lake Adventure

At Lake Adventure the possibilities for fun are endless. Some days you'll want to relax, snooze in the sun, or just be plain lazy. On other days you can enjoy fun features as a lake full of sports fish, a supervised sand swimming beach, children's playground, and our spacious clubhouse with gameroom overlooking beautiful Lake Adventure.

Forest Trail

The mountain bike trails here total over 130km and are supported by a continual programme of trail maintenance and development.

With 4 superb flowing trails and more all weather singletrack than any other centre you simply have to check it out!
